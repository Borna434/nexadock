## NexaDock 0.6.7

### New
- **Shut Down button.** A faint power icon at the left end of the bar shuts the computer down in one click. Apps with unsaved work can still stop it, as with Start › Power › Shut down. Remove or move it like any other widget.

### Update
Installed copies offer this update in Settings › About (and through Pulse). Settings and data are kept.
