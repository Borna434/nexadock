## NexaDock 0.4.1

### New
- **Search (Alt+Space):** a glass search box for apps, files, a calculator (`12*7`, `200*15%`, `sqrt(2)`), unit conversion (`10 km to mi`, `100 c to f`, `2.5 GB to MB`) and the web. Frequently used apps rank higher. Change or turn off the shortcut in Settings › Position & behavior.
- Releases are now built, tested and published automatically.

### Update
Installed copies offer this update in Settings › About (and through Pulse). Settings and data are kept.
