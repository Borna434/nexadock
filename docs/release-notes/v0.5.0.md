## NexaDock 0.5.0

### New
- **Clipboard history:** everything you copy, text and images, in a Clipboard widget and on **Win+Alt+V**. Search, press Enter to paste into the app you were using, pin what you reuse (Ctrl+P). Content that password managers mark as private is never recorded, and everything stays on your PC.
- **Richer Now Playing:** jump within a track with a seek slider, set the volume of the playing app on its own (Spotify quieter than Discord, for example), and the card takes on the colours of the album cover.
- **Colours from your wallpaper:** the accent and the glow follow your desktop wallpaper and change with it (Settings › Appearance).
- **System icons:** network opens the Wi-Fi list, sound opens the sound flyout and shows the real volume, scrolling over them changes the volume, and battery and the gear open Quick Settings.

### Fixes
- The Claude widget now matches Claude's own session reset time. It used to end the session an hour early.
- A rare crash when a window or audio device disappeared while it was being read.
- NexaDock always responds to the installer's request to close, even after a failed start.

### Update
Installed copies offer this update in Settings › About (and through Pulse). Settings and data are kept.
